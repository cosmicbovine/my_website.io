About Us

Your look,
on point.

At On Point Barbershop, we believe every haircut is a chance to feel your best. Located right here in Yuma, AZ, we bring skill, consistency, and a welcoming vibe to every chair.

Whether you're coming in for a fresh fade, a classic taper, a beard shaping, or just a clean up before an important day — our barbers deliver every time. No appointments needed, just walk right in.

We're open seven days a week because we know life doesn't take weekends off.
